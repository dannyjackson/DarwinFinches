# Independent a priori review: Philornis downsi host responses

## Literature basis

The adult fly is free living; its larvae parasitize birds. Early instars inhabit the nostrils and other tissues, while older larvae shelter in nest material and take external blood meals. Tissue destruction, open wounds, severe blood loss and anemia can impair growth and survival. Nestlings are especially vulnerable, although incubating females can also be attacked. These effects motivate potential blood replacement, hemostasis, tissue repair, barrier maintenance, oxygen compensation and nutritional compensation. They do not establish that every underlying molecular pathway has been measured in these birds.

Resistance includes a documented antibody response in nesting female medium ground finches: experimentally parasitized mothers had more fly-binding antibodies, and stronger responses tended to accompany fewer parasites. This did not rescue reproductive success in that experiment. Young nestlings and males did not show equivalent induced responses. A separate comparative experiment found no significant antibody response in either host species. Thus immunity is a sound a priori mechanism, but consistent, effective immune protection cannot be assumed. B-cell and adaptive-immune processes receive strong support; specific cytokine, innate-signaling and inflammatory pathways are generally mechanistic predictions rather than direct findings.

Tolerance can involve energy compensation. Parasitized Galapagos mockingbird nestlings increased begging, associated with greater parental provisioning; sympatric medium ground finches did not show the same compensation. Mockingbird tolerance is environmentally contingent, with food availability and dry years relevant. This supports energetic and nutrient-response processes, while particular metabolic enzymes or endocrine pathways remain supporting or indirect candidates.

Observed or proposed behavioral resistance includes preening or removal/consumption of larvae, nestling repositioning and climbing onto nestmates, parental nest attendance and nest-site or breeding-timing differences that could affect exposure. Evidence is limited and these behaviors are not uniformly effective. Finches also rub Psidium galapageium leaves onto feathers; laboratory extracts repelled adult flies and inhibited larval growth, supporting an antiparasite hypothesis without demonstrating a protective field effect. Permethrin-treated cotton incorporated into nests demonstrates an assisted behavioral control opportunity, rather than an evolved physiological insecticide mechanism. Learning, perception and motor function are therefore potential supporting processes, not blanket evidence for all neural developmental terms.

## Scoring interpretation

Each supplied term was considered separately. Score 5 was reserved for terms directly capturing measured host mechanisms; score 4 includes central components or strong predictions from antibody defense, blood loss and injury; score 3 covers plausible supporting processes; score 2 denotes weak or nonspecific connections; score 1 indicates no specific mechanistic connection. Generic cell machinery was not promoted merely because every response uses it. Reproductive-cell processes, detailed embryonic patterning, virus-specific responses and much RNA/DNA housekeeping generally have no specific link. Bacterial defense is a possible consequence of breached barriers, not evidence that the fly transmits a particular pathogen. Avian platelet annotations were interpreted cautiously in relation to homologous thrombocyte/hemostatic functions. Directional regulatory terms indicate potential involvement, not an assertion that increased or decreased activity is beneficial.

The supplied unlabeled GO:0160240 was resolved through the official ontology as RNA polymerase II transcription initiation surveillance and rated on that identity. No terms or IDs were renamed in the output. No genomic, transcriptomic or gene-expression results from Galapagos birds informed the ratings, and no other rater files were inspected.
