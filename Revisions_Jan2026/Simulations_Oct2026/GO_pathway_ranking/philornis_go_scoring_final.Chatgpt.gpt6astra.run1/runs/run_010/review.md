# Independent a priori review: Philornis downsi host responses

The ratings concern physiological relevance, not evidence that a particular gene or pathway evolved in these birds. I used ecological, behavioral, parasitological and immunological literature, together with general biological knowledge. No genomic, transcriptomic or gene-expression results from Galápagos birds informed the scores. I read only this run's supplied input file on the local machine.

## Mechanisms and evidence

**Blood loss and compensation.** Early larvae inhabit the nostrils and other tissues; later larvae generally shelter in nest material and feed externally on nestling blood, especially at night. Tissue injury and anemia can severely impair growth and survival. In small ground finches, heavier infestation was associated with lower hemoglobin and more immature red blood cells. This directly supports compensatory erythropoiesis and broader hematopoiesis. Iron availability, oxygen supply, cardiovascular compensation and energy production are strong predictions or supporting processes, rather than independently demonstrated molecular defenses.

**Tissue injury and repair.** Larval feeding damages skin and nasal tissues, sometimes leaving persistent enlarged nares. Wounding is directly established. Hemostasis, epithelial barrier restoration, extracellular matrix repair, fibroblast activity and angiogenesis are strong a priori responses to that injury. Inflammation, immune-cell recruitment and clearance of damaged cells are plausible core mechanisms, but the reviewed evidence does not directly validate every cytokine or signaling pathway. Antibacterial responses are relevant principally as potential protection of injured barriers; antiviral pathways have a much weaker specific connection.

**Immune resistance.** Experimental parasite removal demonstrated higher parasite-binding antibody levels in mothers at infested medium ground finch nests. Mothers with higher antibody levels tended to have fewer parasites, but this did not rescue reproductive success. Males and nestlings did not show the same induced response. Another finch–mockingbird experiment found no significant antibody response in either species. Thus antibody-associated adaptive immunity is documented, but protection is incomplete and context dependent. B-cell processes and supporting T-cell and cytokine pathways receive relevance without an assumption that all immune responses are effective or universal. Regulation and restraint of inflammation may also support tolerance.

**Behavioral resistance and avoidance.** Nest cameras documented preening, eating larvae, repositioning or standing above nestmates, and maternal probing of nares and nest material. These observations support sensory detection, motor behavior and associated learning as candidate defenses, without making every neural developmental process specifically relevant. Finches can incorporate supplied insecticide-treated cotton into nests. Rubbing guayabillo leaves on feathers is a proposed natural defense: extracts repel adult flies and inhibit larval growth in laboratory assays, but a protective fitness effect in wild birds was not established.

**Tolerance and resource allocation.** Parasitized mockingbird nestlings increased begging, associated with greater parental provisioning, apparently compensating for parasite damage. Comparable compensation was absent in the studied finches. Mockingbird tolerance weakened in dry years, supporting resource-dependent energetic compensation. Nutrient sensing, food utilization, energy homeostasis and maintenance of growth are therefore relevant. Oxidative stress, proteostasis, autophagy and endocrine adjustment are plausible supporting responses, not directly demonstrated pathway-specific mechanisms in this review.

## Scoring interpretation

I applied the requested 1–5 scale term by term without targeting a distribution. Five denotes a term directly describing a documented response; four denotes a core component or strong physiological prediction; three denotes plausible support; two denotes a weak or indirect connection; one denotes no specific connection. A response can be relevant even if costly, ineffective, or negatively regulated. Generic housekeeping and unrelated embryonic or reproductive processes were not promoted merely because they are necessary for life. Hemostatic terms were interpreted in the context of avian thrombocyte function. The supplied GO:0160240 label contained only its ID; the official ontology identifies it as RNA polymerase II transcription initiation surveillance, rated 1, with its original ID retained.
