# Independent physiological review and scoring rationale

The review used published parasite biology, experimental ecology, immunology and behavioral observations, without using genomic, transcriptomic or gene-expression results from Galápagos birds. Scores concern the biological process itself, not whether a gene might have some unspecified additional function. They do not imply that selection has occurred or that a proposed defense is effective.

## Mechanisms

**Blood loss and tissue injury.** Early Philornis downsi larvae occupy nestling nostrils and other tissues; later larvae reside in nest material and feed externally, particularly at night. Feeding produces lesions and anemia, impairs growth and survival, and can leave persistent naris or beak damage. Consequently, replacing erythrocytes, maintaining oxygen delivery, hemostasis, epithelial repair and wound responses have specific relevance. Erythropoiesis has particularly direct support through the reported reticulocyte response. Hypoxia responses, iron homeostasis, vascular repair and coagulation are strong physiological predictions, rather than all being demonstrated protective adaptations.

**Immune resistance.** Experimental fly removal demonstrated increased fly-binding antibodies in mothers at parasitized medium ground finch nests. Higher maternal antibodies were associated with fewer parasites, but did not prevent brood failure. Males and nestlings did not show the same induced response. Adaptive immunity therefore has direct support; B-cell activation/differentiation and immune regulation are core supporting mechanisms. Innate inflammation, cytokine signaling and complement are credible resistance mechanisms or support for wound defense, but should not be treated as demonstrated effective defenses merely because they are immune terms. Antiviral-specific mechanisms have little specific connection; antibacterial defense is an indirect consequence of open wounds rather than established fly transmission of a bacterial pathogen.

**Tolerance and energetic compensation.** An experimental comparison found mockingbirds tolerated parasite exposure better than medium ground finches. Parasitized mockingbird nestlings begged more, with increased parental provisioning associated with begging; finches lacked that compensatory response in that study. Energy homeostasis consequently has direct relevance. Carbohydrate and lipid metabolism, nutrient sensing and growth control are supporting predictions. A separate adult-female finch experiment did not find parasite-induced corticosterone, hematocrit or body-condition differences, so endocrine stress pathways should not be elevated as though universally demonstrated.

**Behavioral defenses and avoidance.** Published observations include occasional larval removal by preening, nest picking, fly predation and nestling movement; some finches stand more and brood less, potentially compromising nestling thermoregulation. Nest placement or timing may affect exposure, and leaf rubbing has been proposed as self-medication. Incorporating experimentally supplied insecticide-treated cotton reduces parasites, but demonstrates use of provided nesting material rather than proof of an evolved insecticide-detoxification mechanism. Learning, sensory function, circadian timing and thermogenesis can support relevant behaviors, with weaker specificity than the actual behaviors. Generic neural development, embryonic organ patterning, reproduction, RNA processing and chromosome machinery do not acquire strong relevance solely because hosts need those processes to live.

## Interpretation limits

Broad cellular processes receive weak scores unless their named function has a specific physiological connection. A score of 4 can represent a strong prediction without experimental proof; 3 denotes plausible support rather than a demonstrated mechanism. Score 1 for GO:0160240 reflects the supplied entry containing only an identifier and no interpretable biological-process name; its meaning could not be verified in the literature search. No target score distribution was used.
