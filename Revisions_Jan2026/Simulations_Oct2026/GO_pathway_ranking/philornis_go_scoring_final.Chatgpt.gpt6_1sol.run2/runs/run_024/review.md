# Independent a priori mechanism review

The scoring uses ecological, behavioral, parasitological and antibody-assay literature, together with general biological knowledge. No genomic, transcriptomic or gene-expression results from Galápagos birds were used. Each supplied term was judged independently; no score distribution was targeted.

## Host injury and physiological compensation

Early Philornis downsi larvae invade nestling nares and tissues; later larvae shelter in nest material and repeatedly feed externally on blood, often at night. Blood loss, anemia, tissue injury, impaired growth and mortality are well established. These observations motivate strong predictions for erythrocyte production, iron handling, oxygen-deficit responses and hemostasis. Epithelial repair, keratinocyte differentiation, extracellular-matrix repair and vascular restoration are strong mechanistic predictions from wounds, rather than demonstrations that every particular repair pathway has been measured. Oxidative-stress control and cellular survival are plausible supporting processes.

- Fessl, Sinclair and Kleindorfer (2006), “The life-cycle of Philornis downsi ... and its impacts on nestling survival”: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16899139/
- Dudaniec, Kleindorfer and Fessl (2006), “Effects ... on haemoglobin level and nestling survival ...”: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1442-9993.2006.01553.x

## Resistance and its limits

Experiments demonstrated increased fly-binding antibodies in parasitized adult female medium ground finches. The inverse association with parasite abundance was suggestive, not proof of effective protection; reproduction still failed in parasitized nests. Males and nestlings did not show the same induced antibody response. A later finch–mockingbird comparison found no significant antibody-mediated response in either species in that study. Accordingly, adaptive immunity is directly relevant, while B-cell activation/differentiation and T-cell help are core predicted components. Complement, innate recognition, cytokines, inflammation and phagocytosis are plausible resistance or tissue-injury mechanisms; their particular pathways were not thereby experimentally established for this fly. Virus-specific processes have only weak cross-talk relevance, and viral replication processes have no specific connection.

Source:
- Koop et al. (2013), “Experimental demonstration of a parasite-induced immune response in wild birds”: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3930052/

## Tolerance, behavior and longer-term effects

Parasitized Galápagos mockingbird nestlings increased begging, correlated with increased provisioning that appeared to compensate for damage; medium ground finches lacked comparable compensation. This supports energetic homeostasis and nutrient use, with metabolism as supporting physiology. Behavioral responses include standing/agitation, altered brooding and rare larval removal/preening. Reduced brooding can impose thermoregulatory costs. Nest timing/site choice and plant rubbing have been proposed as avoidance defenses, but efficacy should not be assumed. Incorporation of experimentally supplied insecticide-treated cotton reduces parasites; this is assisted behavioral control, not evidence for endogenous detoxification. Persistent naris injury can affect song and foraging, making some sensory, motor and craniofacial processes indirectly relevant without elevating unrelated neural or embryological pathways.

## Application of the scale

Scores of 5 identify processes directly matching documented host responses; 4 identifies core machinery or strong predictions; 3 marks plausible support; 2 marks weak, nonspecific or speculative relationships; 1 marks no specific relationship. Generic housekeeping, unrelated reproductive processes and specialized embryonic patterning do not gain relevance merely because they are necessary for life. Regulatory direction alone does not imply protection: both activation and restraint can participate in balanced host responses. Bird physiology was considered when interpreting mammal-oriented labels such as platelet pathways, through their conserved hemostatic relationship to avian thrombocytes.
