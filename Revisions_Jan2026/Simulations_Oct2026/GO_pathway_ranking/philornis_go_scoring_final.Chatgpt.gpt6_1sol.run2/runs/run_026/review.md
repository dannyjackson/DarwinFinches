# Independent physiological relevance review

## Evidence and mechanisms

Philornis downsi adults are free living; the damaging stages are larvae. Early larvae occupy the nares and other nestling tissues; older larvae occupy nest material and feed on nestlings, often at night. Blood removal, tissue consumption, open wounds and persistent nasal deformation provide direct links to blood-loss responses, oxygen delivery and tissue repair. Growth and survival can be severely impaired. Erythropoiesis, iron handling, hemostasis, hypoxia responses, epithelial maintenance and vascular repair are strong physiological predictions; those particular compensatory mechanisms should not be described as experimentally demonstrated merely because blood loss was demonstrated.

Resistance includes parasite-specific humoral immunity. Koop et al. experimentally found increased P. downsi-binding antibodies in female medium ground finches; stronger responses tended to accompany fewer parasites, but failed to rescue reproductive success. Males and young nestlings did not show equivalent induced responses in that experiment. Antibody-associated inflammation, defensive peptides and itching-mediated behavior are proposed mechanisms, rather than uniformly demonstrated protection. B-cell processes are therefore strongly relevant; general innate immunity, inflammatory signaling and leukocyte functions are also plausible, with distinctions between broad pathways and particular cytokines.

Behavioral observations include nest sanitation, preening and allopreening, nestling agitation and attempts to move away from larvae; brooding females may stand more and brood less. Responses differ among species, ages and studies, and observed behavior need not be effective resistance. Provision of permethrin-treated cotton demonstrated that finches can incorporate treated material and substantially reduce infestation, but does not establish innate insecticide production or detoxification as a natural defense.

Tolerance can involve compensation rather than fewer parasites. Experimental work found that parasitized mockingbird nestlings begged more and received more provisioning, while finches lacked comparable compensation. Multi-year work subsequently showed that mockingbird tolerance varies with rainfall and provisioning. Thus nutrient sensing, energy balance and metabolic support are relevant, whereas each individual metabolic or neural pathway requires its own narrower judgment. Growth impairment does not automatically implicate all embryonic developmental pathways.

## Scoring interpretation

Scores concern prospective association, not evidence that any listed pathway is selected. Direct response categories receive the highest scores; essential components or strong predictions receive 4; supporting physiology receives 3. General cellular infrastructure, nonspecific signaling and remote behavioral machinery generally receive 2, while unrelated organogenesis, reproduction, chromosome maintenance and RNA-processing specifics generally receive 1. Negative regulation can be relevant through restraint of inflammatory injury or altered resistance. Virus-specific defenses have no special link to this blood-feeding fly. Platelet-named terms are interpreted cautiously because avian hemostasis uses thrombocytes.
