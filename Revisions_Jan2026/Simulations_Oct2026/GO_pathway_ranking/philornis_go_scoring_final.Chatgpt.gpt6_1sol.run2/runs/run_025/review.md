# A priori physiological relevance review

This rating uses published ecological, parasitological, behavioral and immunological work and general biological knowledge. No genomic, transcriptomic or gene-expression results from Galápagos birds were used. Every input term was judged individually; scores were not chosen to meet a distribution.

## Mechanisms and evidence

**Injury, blood loss and compensation.** Early P. downsi larvae invade nestling nares and other tissues; later larvae occupy nest material and feed externally on hosts. Tissue consumption and blood removal cause wounds, anemia, impaired growth and mortality. These observations make erythrocyte replacement, iron handling, oxygen availability, hemostasis and tissue repair strong candidate host responses. The latter mechanisms are predictions where the studies establish injury rather than directly demonstrate the particular repair pathway.
- Fessl, Sinclair & Kleindorfer (2006), life cycle and effects on nestlings: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16899139/
- Dudaniec & Kleindorfer (2006), review of Philornis effects, including the reported association of infestation with reduced hemoglobin and increased reticulocytes in small ground finches: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/233757508_Effects_of_the_parasitic_flies_of_the_genus_Philornis_Diptera_Muscidae_on_birds
- Koop et al. (2011), experimental fitness consequences and growth effects: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3092749/
Persistent naris injury can also alter song, but that does not make every neural development or synaptic process a specific defense mechanism. See Kleindorfer et al. (2019): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6571467/

**Immune resistance.** An experimental study found greater fly-binding antibody levels in mothers at infested medium ground finch nests, with higher antibody levels associated with fewer parasites. Males and nestlings did not show equivalent induced responses, and maternal immunity did not rescue reproductive success. Humoral/adaptive immunity therefore has direct support. B-cell processes and supporting T-cell activation are strong candidates. Inflammatory signaling, leukocyte recruitment, complement and skin defenses are mechanistically reasonable predictions, not proof that each particular cytokine pathway confers resistance. Antibacterial defenses may help after wounding; virus-specific processes have much weaker relevance.
- Koop et al. (2013): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3930052/

**Tolerance and energy compensation.** Experimental comparison found that parasitized Galápagos mockingbird nestlings increased begging, associated with greater parental provisioning and apparent compensation for damage. Medium ground finches lacked the same compensatory behavioral response; neither species showed a significant antibody-mediated response in that experiment. Thus nutritional state, energy balance, growth maintenance and feeding-related behavior are relevant, while broad metabolic machinery receives lower scores than a specific compensatory mechanism.
- Knutie et al. (2016), published online study of mockingbird tolerance: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28792593/

**Behavioral resistance and avoidance.** Nest sanitation, larval removal, preening and avoidance movements have been observed or proposed, but effective responses are inconsistent and often limited. Finch feather-rubbing with Psidium leaves is documented; extracts repel adult flies and inhibit larval growth experimentally, although protection of free-living birds by rubbing remains unproven. Finches can also incorporate supplied insecticide-treated cotton into nests, demonstrating a management-assisted behavior rather than endogenous insecticide production.
- Cimadom et al. (2016): https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27721475/
- Knutie et al. (2014): https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24801182/
- McNew & Clayton (2018), broader synthesis: https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-ento-020117-043103

## Scoring interpretation

Directly represented demonstrated mechanisms receive 5; strong components or predictions receive 4; supportive metabolism, stress physiology and cell maintenance typically receive 3 when a clear mechanistic connection exists. Ubiquitous molecular machinery and nonspecific behavioral/neural pathways generally receive 2; unrelated reproductive, embryonic or other specialized processes receive 1 where no specific connection is evident. Scores indicate potential relevance, not demonstrated adaptation, benefit, or direction of effect. Avian coagulation is relevant despite mammalian-style platelet terminology. GO:0160240 has only an identifier as its supplied name; its identity could not be verified in targeted web searches, so it receives 1 rather than an invented functional interpretation.
