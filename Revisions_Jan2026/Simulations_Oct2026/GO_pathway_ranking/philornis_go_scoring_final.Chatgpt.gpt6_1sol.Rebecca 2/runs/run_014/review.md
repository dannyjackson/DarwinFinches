# Independent literature basis for ratings

Only the supplied input terms were read locally. Scores use published ecological, behavioral, immunological and physiological work plus general biology; no host genomic, transcriptomic, gene-expression, methylation, or selection results were used. Molecular studies encountered in search results were excluded. No target score distribution was imposed.

## Effects and candidate mechanisms

Philornis downsi adults are free-living. Early larvae invade nestling nares and tissue; later larvae hide in nest material and feed externally on blood, particularly at night. Feeding causes tissue destruction, substantial blood loss, anemia, reduced development and frequent mortality. Persistent naris and beak damage can compromise later feeding and other beak functions. These observations make blood replacement, iron availability, oxygen homeostasis, hemostasis, epithelial repair and maintenance of tissue barriers strong a priori response mechanisms. They do not establish that every constituent molecular pathway has been experimentally demonstrated as a defense.

Resistance has some direct immunological support: experimental parasite removal showed a fly-binding antibody response in adult female medium ground finches, with higher antibody levels tending to accompany lower parasite numbers. However, this did not rescue offspring in that experiment, and males and nestlings did not show corresponding responses. Other comparisons found no significant antibody response. Adaptive immunity receives direct relevance; B-cell function, helper T-cell function and immune regulation are components or predictions. Inflammation, innate recognition, complement and phagocytosis are plausible mechanisms rather than proven effective anti-fly defenses. Antiviral and intracellular infection mechanisms have no specific demonstrated fly link; bacterial defenses are only indirectly relevant through damaged barriers.

Tolerance can operate through nutrition and energy compensation. Parasitized Galapagos mockingbird nestlings increased begging, associated with increased parental provisioning, unlike medium ground finches in the same comparison. Across years, mockingbird tolerance was lower during low-rainfall conditions, consistent with resource limitation restricting compensation. Energy homeostasis is therefore strongly relevant, and glucose/lipid/amino-acid metabolism, nutrient responses and aerobic metabolism are supporting processes. Oxidative stress, cell survival and growth regulation are plausible downstream mechanisms, not demonstrated specific molecular defenses.

Behavioral resistance includes occasional removal or consumption of larvae, preening, nest sanitation and postural avoidance; its effectiveness is limited or inconsistent. Finch application of Psidium galapageium leaves and experimentally demonstrated repellency suggest behavioral avoidance, without proving naturally effective protection of nestlings. Human-assisted incorporation of insecticide-treated cotton establishes a useful behavioral control route, not endogenous pesticide detoxification. Learning and sensory processing can support behavior, but generic neural development and specific synaptic processes remain weak or nonspecific links.

A proposed maternal corticosterone-mediated mechanism was tested without a parasite-treatment effect; stress and hormone pathways remain plausible contextual links, not established direct defenses. Specific embryonic organ patterning, reproduction, meiosis, vision-development and unrelated infection processes generally lack a specific link. Generic transcription, translation, trafficking and proteostasis were usually weak links unless the term specified a relevant mechanism. GO:0160240 was identified in public annotations as RNA polymerase II transcription initiation surveillance and rated as nonspecific transcriptional support; its input identity was preserved.

## Scale application

5: direct documented response mechanism. 4: core component or strong physiological prediction. 3: plausible supporting or downstream process. 2: weak, generic or speculative support. 1: no specific link. Closely related regulatory terms were judged by the same biological rationale; either positive or negative regulation may support homeostasis, and a score does not assert a beneficial direction of selection or demonstrated effectiveness.
