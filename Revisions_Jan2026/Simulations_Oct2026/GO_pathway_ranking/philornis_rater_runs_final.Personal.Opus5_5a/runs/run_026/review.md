# Philornis downsi and Galápagos land birds: mechanisms used for scoring

## What the larvae do
- Adult flies lay eggs in nests; 1st-instar larvae live in nestling nares/ear canals, later instars live in nest material and feed externally on nestling blood and tissue at night. This causes blood loss (lower haemoglobin), open wounds and tissue damage, naris enlargement/beak deformation (which later alters song and mating signals), reduced growth, and very high nestling mortality. Brooding females are also bitten.
- Parasitism also changes adult behaviour/physiology (corticosterone, provisioning effort) and nestling behaviour (begging, posture) predicts naris damage.

## Host defenses documented or proposed
- **Resistance (adaptive/humoral):** finches produce P. downsi-specific IgY antibodies; antibody titres correlate negatively with parasite load (Huber et al. 2010; Koop et al. 2013). Inflammatory/innate responses at bite sites and cutaneous immunity are a strong prediction.
- **Tolerance:** maintenance of haemoglobin/erythropoiesis, haemostasis/coagulation, wound healing and tissue repair, compensatory growth and energy allocation; tolerance varies with food availability (Knutie et al.). Galápagos mockingbirds tolerate the parasite better than finches. Hypoxia responses and iron homeostasis follow from anaemia.
- **Behavioural:** nestling begging/posture, parental provisioning and brooding; self-fumigation with treated cotton is an experimental (human-assisted) defense. Neural/learning terms considered only weakly related.

## Scoring rationale
5 = haemostasis/platelets, wound healing, antibody/B-cell immunity, erythropoiesis, immune response; 4 = inflammation, innate immunity, complement, T cells, skin/epidermis, iron homeostasis, hypoxia, pain; 3 = cytokine/NF-kB/TLR/JAK-STAT signalling, leukocyte chemotaxis, phagocytosis, angiogenesis, ECM/collagen, TGF-b/EGF repair signalling, oxidative stress, glucocorticoid signalling, nutrient/starvation responses; 2 = beak/bone development, growth/metabolism, general stress, apoptosis, antiviral immunity; 1 = generic housekeeping, neural development, meiosis, etc.
