# Philornis downsi and Galapagos land birds: mechanisms used for scoring (run_005)

## What the larvae do to hosts
- Adult flies lay eggs in nests; first-instar larvae enter nestlings' nares and beak cavities, later instars live in nest material and feed externally on nestling blood and tissue at night (semi-hematophagous). Effects: blood loss and reduced hemoglobin (anemia), open wounds, enlarged nares and deformed beaks persisting into adulthood (altering song and potentially mate recognition/hybridization), reduced growth, and high nestling mortality (often >50%; up to ~100% in some seasons/species).
- Affects many Darwin's finch species, Galapagos mockingbirds, warbler-finches, yellow warblers and other passerines.

## Host defenses documented or proposed
- **Resistance (immune):** nestlings and adult females produce P. downsi-specific IgY antibodies; experimental parasite removal shows a parasite-induced antibody response; antibody levels in some studies negatively related to parasite load. Inflammation and wound responses at feeding sites are implied.
- **Tolerance:** maintaining growth/survival per unit parasite load via blood replacement (erythropoiesis, iron), hemostasis and wound repair, and increased parental food provisioning/nestling begging; food availability (e.g., urban areas with human food) improves nestling survival under parasitism; hemoglobin and growth measures used as tolerance metrics.
- **Behavioral:** parental (female) nest attendance/brooding relates to parasite numbers; nestling begging changes in parasitized nests; finches incorporate permethrin-treated cotton into nests (self-fumigation), and use of aromatic plant material/learned nest-building behaviors proposed; adult-female and nestling preening/grooming plausible.
- Secondary predictions: hypoxia/oxidative stress from anemia, stress-hormone and energy-allocation responses, beak/bone tissue remodeling.

## Scoring logic
5 = immune/antibody (adaptive, B cell), inflammation, wound healing, hemostasis/coagulation, erythropoiesis/hematopoiesis, defense response.
4 = core components (T/B cell signaling, cytokines/chemokines, innate immunity, platelets, iron homeostasis, hypoxia, keratinocyte/epidermis repair).
3 = supporting (NF-kB/TLR/JAK-STAT, angiogenesis, ECM/collagen, oxidative stress, bone remodeling, nutrition/energy homeostasis, learning/cognition, pain).
2 = speculative (generic stress/hormone, metabolism, antiviral/antibacterial, MAPK, apoptosis signaling, thermogenesis). 1 = housekeeping/developmental/neural/etc.
