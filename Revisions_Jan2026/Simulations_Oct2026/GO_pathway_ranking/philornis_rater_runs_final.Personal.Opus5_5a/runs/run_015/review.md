# P. downsi host-response review (run_015)

## What the larvae do
- First instars live in nestling nares and ear canals and eat keratin and tissue, which causes naris and beak deformation. Later instars feed externally on blood and tissue at night. The result is blood loss (lower hemoglobin), reduced growth and high nestling mortality. Larvae may also feed on brooding or incubating females.
- No measurable effect on corticosterone, body condition or hematocrit in breeding female medium ground finches.

## Host defenses documented or proposed
- **Resistance (adaptive):** brooding females carry P. downsi-specific IgY, and higher levels go with lower parasite intensity. Nestling antibody responses are weak.
- **Resistance (innate):** pro-inflammatory responses are proposed as an innate resistance route in urban nestlings. Inflammation and wound response at larval feeding sites are general features of Philornis myiasis.
- **Tolerance:** mockingbirds tolerate the parasite. They keep fledging success despite a roughly 40% hemoglobin drop, possibly by compensating for blood loss through erythropoiesis and red blood cell replacement. Diet and protein (urban, higher δ15N) are linked to lower parasite load. Energy and glucose balance are also involved: unparasitized mockingbird nestlings had higher plasma glucose.
- **Behavioral:** begging and parental provisioning compensation (seen in mockingbirds, not in finches or small tree finches); females standing upright or brooding less; possible allopreening; incorporating permethrin-treated cotton into nests (self-fumigation).

## Scoring logic
- 5 = immune, inflammatory, wound-healing, adaptive/antibody (B cell) and erythropoiesis/hemopoiesis terms.
- 4 = core immune signaling (NF-kB, TLR, cytokines, TNF, IL-1, T and B cell activation), hypoxia response, iron homeostasis, complement, coagulation and platelets.
- 3 = supporting processes: oxidative stress, angiogenesis, ECM and collagen, keratinocyte and epidermis, glucose homeostasis, chemotaxis, phagocytosis, pain.
- 2 = weak links: general stress, growth factors, metabolism and energy, bone and beak development, learning and memory (behavior).
- 1 = everything else.
