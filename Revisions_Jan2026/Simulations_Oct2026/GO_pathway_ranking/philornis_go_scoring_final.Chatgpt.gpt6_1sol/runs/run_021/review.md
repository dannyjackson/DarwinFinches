# A priori physiological relevance review

The ratings use published natural-history, experimental, physiological and behavioral evidence plus general biology. They assess potential association with a host response, not evidence that a particular gene or process actually evolved in these birds. No genomic, transcriptomic or gene-expression findings were used. Search hits involving those approaches were excluded.

## Mechanisms and evidence

* **Blood and tissue loss.** Early larvae occupy nestling nares and other tissues; later larvae feed externally, largely at night, from the nest substrate. Tissue injury, anemia, impaired growth and mortality make blood replacement and oxygen delivery central response candidates. Fessl, Sinclair & Kleindorfer (2006), *The life-cycle of Philornis downsi ... and its impacts on nestling survival*: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16899139/ . Dudaniec, Kleindorfer & Fessl (2006) associated infestation with lower hemoglobin and increased immature red cells, supporting compensatory erythropoiesis: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1442-9993.2006.01553.x . Hypoxia responses, iron handling and hemostasis are strong physiological predictions; their specific molecular pathways were not measured by these studies.
* **Immune resistance.** Experimentally parasitized medium ground finch mothers produced fly-binding antibodies; stronger responses tended to accompany fewer parasites, but did not rescue offspring survival. Nestlings and males did not show the same response. Antibody/adaptive immunity is therefore documented, while lymphocyte activation, complement and inflammatory regulation are mechanistic predictions with varying specificity. Koop et al. (2013): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3930052/ .
* **Nutritional tolerance.** Parasitized Galapagos mockingbird nestlings increased begging, associated with greater parental provisioning and compensation for parasite injury; medium ground finches did not exhibit the same effective compensation. Knutie et al. (2016): https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28792593/ . Tolerance weakened in dry years, linking resource availability to host compensation (McNew et al. 2019): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6408884/ . Energy balance and nutrient use are relevant; generic biosynthetic machinery is less specific.
* **Behavioral resistance and avoidance.** Video observations include grooming, larval removal/consumption and nestlings climbing over nestmates, but these defenses are limited and age dependent. O'Connor, Robertson & Kleindorfer (2010): https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/oryx/article/video-analysis-of-hostparasite-interactions-in-nests-of-darwins-finches/BF956C9751763DAFAEE36345498A4A01 . Birds use supplied insecticide-treated cotton, demonstrating a behavior-assisted conservation intervention rather than evolved insecticide production: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24801182/ . Leaf rubbing with Psidium galapageium and extract repellency support proposed self-medication, without establishing field protection: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5056383/ .
* **Repair and lasting damage.** Nasal injury can persist and affect adult foraging and song. Epithelial repair, extracellular matrix remodeling and vascular repair are strong predictions from injury, rather than directly demonstrated molecular defenses. Phenotypic evidence: https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2435.14061 and https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6571467/ . Broad neural, developmental and cellular processes receive lower scores unless their own meaning supplies a closer connection.

## Scoring interpretation

Score 5 denotes a process directly capturing a documented response (including antibody immunity, erythrocyte replacement, or response to injury). Score 4 denotes a core component or strong prediction; 3 a plausible supporting process; 2 an indirect or speculative connection; 1 no specific connection. A negative regulatory term can be relevant because controlling excessive defense or repair is also a host response. Antibacterial responses were treated as secondary wound-related possibilities, and antiviral specificity generally remained weak. Generic transcription, chromosome maintenance and unrelated embryonic or reproductive processes were not elevated merely because all responses require living cells. Platelet terms were interpreted cautiously as hemostatic functions with analogous avian thrombocyte involvement, not evidence that birds have mammalian platelets.

Every input GO ID was retained in order, with one independent judgment per term and no target score distribution.
