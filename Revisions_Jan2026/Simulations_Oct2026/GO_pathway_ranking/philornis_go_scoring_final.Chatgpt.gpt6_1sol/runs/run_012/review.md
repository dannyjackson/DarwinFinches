# A priori host-response review

The ratings use the supplied GO terms, non-omic Philornis literature, and general biological knowledge. No host genomic, transcriptomic, gene-expression, or selection results informed them. A high rating indicates a plausible response mechanism, not evidence that selection occurred or that a defense succeeds. Every term was considered at its stated specificity; generic cellular machinery received lower ratings than a directly relevant physiological process.

## Mechanisms and evidence

**Blood and tissue loss.** Early larvae inhabit the nares and other tissues; later larvae feed on nestlings from the nest material. Hematophagy and tissue injury cause anemia, impaired growth, and mortality. This supports red-cell replacement as a direct compensatory mechanism, and iron handling, hypoxia response, hemostasis, epithelial repair, vascular repair, and extracellular-matrix remodeling as strong predictions or supporting processes. The latter are not all demonstrated molecular responses in this system. Sources: Fessl, Sinclair & Kleindorfer (2006), https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16899139/ ; Koop et al. (2011), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3092749/ . Dudaniec & Kleindorfer's review reports increased reticulocytes accompanying low hemoglobin in parasitized small ground finches, supporting compensatory erythropoiesis: https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Rachael-Dudaniec/publication/233757508_Effects_of_the_parasitic_flies_of_the_genus_Philornis_Diptera_Muscidae_on_birds/links/09e4150b41a57cd318000000/Effects-of-the-parasitic-flies-of-the-genus-Philornis-Diptera-Muscidae-on-birds.pdf .

**Immune resistance.** An experiment found parasite-binding antibodies elevated in mothers from infested medium ground-finch nests, with higher antibody levels associated with fewer larvae. This did not rescue fledging, and comparable induced responses were absent in fathers and nestlings. Adaptive immunity therefore has direct support; B-cell mechanisms, T-cell help, inflammatory signaling, innate defenses, complement, and immune-cell trafficking have varying degrees of mechanistic plausibility. Antibacterial defense can protect injured barriers, but secondary infection is a prediction, not an established principal cause here. Virus-specific processes have little specific support. Source: Koop et al. (2013), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3930052/ .

**Nutritional tolerance.** Galapagos mockingbird nestlings increased begging under parasitism, associated with greater parental feeding and maintained fitness; medium ground finches did not show equivalent compensation in that comparison. Mockingbird tolerance also varied with rainfall and provisioning across years. These observations support energy balance and feeding-related behavior; nutrient transport, carbohydrate and fat use, growth regulation, and oxidative protection are supporting predictions, rather than demonstrated pathway changes. Sources: Knutie et al. (2016), https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27220210/ ; McNew et al. (2019), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6408884/ .

**Behavioral resistance and avoidance.** Larval removal, occasional preening/consumption, and avoidance movements have been observed, although effective behavioral resistance is generally limited. Nest placement and reproductive timing are proposed avoidance routes. Birds can incorporate experimentally supplied insecticide-treated cotton into nests; this demonstrates a useful behavior under intervention, not evolved insecticide metabolism. General neural development or synaptic machinery is only indirectly connected. Sources: McNew & Clayton (2018), https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-ento-020117-043103 ; Knutie et al. (2014), https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24801182/ .

## Scoring interpretation

5 identifies directly represented mechanisms, particularly adaptive defense and erythrocyte replacement. 4 identifies core immune, wound-response, barrier, coagulation, and hypoxia mechanisms or strong predictions. 3 identifies plausible supporting physiology. 2 identifies remote, generic, or speculative connections. 1 identifies processes with no specific connection, including many embryological, meiotic, and RNA-processing terms. Regulatory direction alone was not treated as evidence of benefit: both positive and negative immune regulation can matter. The supplied unnamed GO:0160240 was verified as RNA polymerase II transcription initiation surveillance and retained unchanged in the score file; https://amigo.geneontology.org/amigo/term/GO%3A0160240 .
