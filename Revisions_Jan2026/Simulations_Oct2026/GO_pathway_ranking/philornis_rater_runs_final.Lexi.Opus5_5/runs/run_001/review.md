# Literature review: host responses to *Philornis downsi* (run_001)

## What the larvae do
- First-instar larvae live in the nares and beak cavities of nestlings. Later instars live in the nest base and feed on blood and tissue at night, which suggests repeated feeding wounds and repeated blood loss.
- Parasitized nestlings have lower hemoglobin, are smaller (mass and tarsus) and fledge less often. Mortality can reach 100%.
- The nares and beak can be deformed. This has been linked to altered song and mating signals.
- Whether wounds become infected is unknown. Arbovirus vectoring by the larvae has been proposed but little studied.

## Host defenses
- **Immune resistance:** Adult female medium ground finches make *P. downsi*-specific IgY antibodies, and antibody titre is inversely related to parasite load. Nestlings show little or no antibody response, and the maternal response did not improve nestling survival.
- **Tolerance:** Parasitized mockingbird nestlings beg more and parents feed them more, so the flies do not reduce their fitness. Finches show no rise in begging, provisioning or plasma glucose, and the flies do reduce their fitness.
- **Physiological stress:** Corticosterone in nesting females was unchanged.
- **Behavioral defenses:** Nest placement and avoidance. Nestlings may crawl over siblings to avoid larvae. Preening against larvae is rare. Females spend more time standing than brooding, which may change nest temperature. Finches use cotton treated with insecticide ("self-fumigation") and possibly guava leaves.

## How the review shaped the scores
- **High (4–5):**
  - antibody and B/T-cell adaptive immunity
  - general immune, inflammatory and cytokine responses
  - leukocyte recruitment and complement
  - hemostasis (coagulation, platelets)
  - erythropoiesis and iron homeostasis, as compensation for blood loss
  - wound healing
- **Moderate (3):**
  - innate immune signalling (TLR, NF-kB, JAK-STAT)
  - oxidative stress and hypoxia
  - skin and keratinocyte repair, ECM and angiogenesis
  - nutrient and energy homeostasis, glucose, starvation and IGF (tolerance and growth)
  - nociception
- **Low (2):** antiviral and antibacterial terms, hormones and stress, thermoregulation, skeletal and beak growth, learning and hearing, xenobiotic response.
- **1:** all other housekeeping and developmental processes.
