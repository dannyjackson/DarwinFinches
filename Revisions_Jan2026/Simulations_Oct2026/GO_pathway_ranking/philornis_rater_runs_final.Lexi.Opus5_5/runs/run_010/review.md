# Run 010 literature review: host responses to Philornis downsi

## What the larvae do
- P. downsi larvae live in nest material and feed on the blood and tissue of nestlings at night. Early instars go into the nares and later instars feed externally. The result is blood loss (lower hemoglobin), tissue wounds, deformed nares and beaks, slower growth and high nestling mortality. There are also effects on song through beak and naris damage. Sources: Fessl et al.; Koop et al.; Wikipedia summary; Galligan & Kleindorfer.

## Documented or proposed host defenses
1. **Resistance via adaptive immunity.** Adult female finches and nestlings produce P. downsi-specific antibodies (IgY). Koop et al. 2013 showed experimentally that the antibody response is induced by the parasite, and higher antibody titres were linked to fewer parasites. This supports B cell activation, B cell differentiation and the adaptive and humoral immune response.
2. **Innate immunity and inflammation at feeding wounds.** Proposed: inflammation, cytokine signalling, wound healing and re-epithelialisation of skin and nares.
3. **Tolerance to blood loss.** Galápagos mockingbirds tolerate the fly: nestlings survive similar parasite loads while finches suffer. This is attributed to compensating for blood loss and to parental compensation. Predicted processes: erythropoiesis, hemopoiesis, iron homeostasis, hemostasis/coagulation, platelet activation and the hypoxia response.
4. **Physiological stress and energetics.** Studies tested effects on corticosterone. Parasitised nestlings need resources to make up for blood and tissue loss, so metabolic, glucose and growth pathways are plausible but indirect.
5. **Behaviour.** Changes in begging and parental provisioning, nest sanitation and preening, and self-fumigation (rubbing Psidium galbanum leaves on feathers) have been documented. These map only weakly onto specific GO processes such as learning, memory and sensory processes.
6. **Gut microbiota and immune development** in nestlings have been studied as factors in resistance. This gives a weak link to antibacterial defence.

## How terms were scored
- 5: antibody-based adaptive immune response, immune response, B cell activation, erythrocyte differentiation.
- 4: B cell receptor signalling and proliferation, hemopoiesis, iron homeostasis, coagulation and platelet processes, wound healing, inflammatory response, cytokine signalling, T cell activation, innate immunity.
- 3: downstream immune signalling (NF-kB, TNF, IL-6, TLR, JAK-STAT, complement, phagocytosis, chemotaxis), angiogenesis, extracellular matrix and skin repair, oxidative stress, hypoxia, energy homeostasis and response to starvation.
- 2: antiviral immunity, general growth and metabolism, hormone signalling, behavioural and neural processes, adhesion and migration, apoptosis and autophagy.
- 1: all other terms, such as housekeeping, RNA processing, cell cycle and unrelated development.
