# Philornis downsi and Galapagos land birds: mechanisms used for scoring

## What the larvae do
- P. downsi adults lay eggs in nests; larvae are semi-hematophagous, feeding on nestling blood and tissue (externally and in the nares), causing blood loss, reduced hemoglobin, open wounds/tissue damage, enlarged/deformed nares and beak, reduced growth, and high nestling mortality (often >50%; up to 100% in some nests). Adult females brooding are also bitten.
- Secondary effects: anemia-related physiological stress, energetic/nutritional cost, possible secondary infection of wounds, altered begging and parental provisioning, stress hormones in parents.

## Host defenses documented or proposed
- **Resistance (immune):** parasite-specific IgY antibody responses in nestlings and brooding females; higher female antibody titres associated with fewer parasites (Huber et al. 2010; Koop et al. 2013). Implies B-cell activation, antibody production, T-helper support, complement, and inflammation at feeding sites (as for other blood-feeding ectoparasites).
- **Tolerance:** Galapagos mockingbirds tolerate parasites; parasitized nestlings maintain growth/survival, linked to increased parental provisioning (Knutie et al. 2016). Proposed tolerance pathways: erythropoiesis/blood replacement, iron homeostasis, hemostasis/coagulation against anticoagulant saliva, wound healing and tissue repair, nutritional/energy homeostasis, hypoxia response.
- **Behavioral:** nest attendance and female in-nest behavior (Cimadom et al.; O'Connor et al. video studies), nestling defensive movements/preening, parental provisioning compensation (Green Warbler-Finch study).
- **Stress physiology:** corticosterone in breeding finches tested experimentally (Knutie et al.).

## Scoring logic
5 = antibody/adaptive immune response; 4 = core immune, inflammation, hemostasis, erythropoiesis/iron, wound healing; 3 = cytokine/innate signaling, secondary bacterial defense, oxidative/hypoxic stress, skin/ECM/angiogenesis repair, starvation/nutrient response; 2 = weakly linked metabolism, growth, behavior, generic stress/signaling, bone/beak development; 1 = everything else.
