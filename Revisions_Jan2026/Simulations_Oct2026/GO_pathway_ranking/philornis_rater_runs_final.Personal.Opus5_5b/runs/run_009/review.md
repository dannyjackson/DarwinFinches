# Philornis downsi and host responses: review summary (run_009)

## What the larvae do
- Larvae are semi-hematophagous nest parasites. Early instars feed inside the nares, and later instars feed externally on the blood and tissue of nestlings (and brooding females). This causes wounds, lesions, subcutaneous bleeding, enlarged or deformed nares and beaks, reduced mass and feather growth, lower hemoglobin (about 0.8 units lost per larva), and high nestling mortality (often above 50%, sometimes 100%).
- Survivors may keep enlarged nares into adulthood, which may affect song and mate choice (untested).

## Host defenses documented or proposed
1. **Adaptive/humoral resistance:** adult female medium ground finches produce *P. downsi*-specific antibodies (IgY, measured by ELISA). Titers are higher in parasitized nests, which is an experimental demonstration of a parasite-induced immune response. Whether the response is protective (fitness consequences) is unresolved. No significant antibody response was found in a later mockingbird/finch comparison.
2. **Innate/inflammatory resistance:** proposed as a defense against the feeding wounds of blood-feeding ectoparasites. Pathology of philornid myiasis in other hosts involves local inflammation and tissue damage.
3. **Tolerance:** Galápagos mockingbirds tolerate the fly. Parasitized nestlings beg more and parents provision more, which offsets the cost. Tolerance of blood loss (red blood cell replacement, recovery of hemoglobin) and nutrition/protein intake are proposed tolerance routes. Hemostasis and wound repair are strong a priori predictions.
4. **Behavioral:** females stand, probe the nest base, preen nestlings and eat larvae. Nestlings stand on siblings and preen. Finches rub *Psidium galapageium* leaves on their feathers, and the leaves repel adult flies and reduce larval weight gain.
5. **Stress physiology:** corticosterone, body condition and hematocrit of breeding females did not differ with parasitism.
6. **Nest microclimate:** larvae develop faster in warmer nests.

## Scoring logic
- 5: adaptive immune response and B cell activation (antibody production documented).
- 4: general immune/defense, B/T cell signalling, innate immunity, inflammation, cytokines, complement, wound healing, hemostasis/coagulation/platelets, erythropoiesis/hemopoiesis, iron homeostasis (blood-loss tolerance).
- 3: regulators of inflammation, leukocyte chemotaxis/phagocytosis, NF-kB/JAK-STAT, hypoxia/oxidative stress, epidermis/skin/keratinocytes, ECM/collagen/angiogenesis (tissue repair), energy and nutrient homeostasis (tolerance via provisioning), pain perception.
- 2: antiviral/antibacterial immunity, general stress and hormone signalling, metabolism, learning/memory (behavioral defenses), xenobiotic response (plant volatiles), bone/beak development, and similar indirect links.
- 1: housekeeping, development, neural, reproductive and other processes with no specific link.

Note: one abstract (urban vs non-urban nestlings) reported expression results. Per the rules, those expression results were not used to set any score.
